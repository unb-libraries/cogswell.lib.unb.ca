<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Although George Burroughs often used a knife,</span>
<span class="line">As farmers must, to castrate or to kill,</span>
<span class="line">He took no pleasure in the touch of steel.</span>
<span class="line">As one who loved to feel things come to life</span>
<span class="line">Beneath his hands, to end the patient strife</span>
<span class="line">Of calving heifers pleased him best of all.</span>
<span class="line">He liked the springtime better than the fall,</span>
<span class="line">But all his farm was fruitful save his wife.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">They say that when the doctor came last Spring</span>
<span class="line">And said that he could never have a child,</span>
<span class="line">Although he neither stormed nor acted wild,</span>
<span class="line">He would not let the seed-drill work his lands</span>
<span class="line">But walked the barren fields himself, sowing</span>
<span class="line">The seed broadcast with quick jerks of his hands.</span></p>
