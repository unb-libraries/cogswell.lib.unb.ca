<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Out in an orchard evening</span>
<span class="line">They walked without a word,</span>
<span class="line">And apple buds in moonlight</span>
<span class="line">Above them swelled and stirred.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Out in an orchard evening</span>
<span class="line">They walked through apple bowers</span>
<span class="line">And on them fell the honey</span>
<span class="line">Of gently swaying flowers.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Now back from orchard evening</span>
<span class="line">They come without a word,</span>
<span class="line">But deadly hangs the silence</span>
<span class="line">Between them like a sword,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And lips that kiss at parting</span>
<span class="line">Are now a hail-hard shower</span>
<span class="line">To bruise and shred and mangle</span>
<span class="line">The remnants of a flower.</span></p>
