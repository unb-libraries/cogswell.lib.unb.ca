<p class="stanza"><span class="line">As winter apples stored in bins</span>
<span class="line">A fragrant heat keep on</span>
<span class="line">And sweeten in their wrinkled skins</span>
<span class="line">Though suns of seasons gone,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The loves I stole from summer's tree</span>
<span class="line">And plucked before their prime</span>
<span class="line">In winter bins of memory</span>
<span class="line">Grow sweeter all the time.</span></p>
