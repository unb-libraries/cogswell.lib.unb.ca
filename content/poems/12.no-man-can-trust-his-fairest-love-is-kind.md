<p class="stanza"><span class="line">No man can trust his fairest love is kind,</span>
<span class="line">For art has alchemy so subtly bold</span>
<span class="line">It turns base metal into seeming gold;</span>
<span class="line">And faith in Nature's goodness is as blind.</span>
<span class="line">For under ripeness of the apple's rind</span>
<span class="line">Corruption often soils exploring teeth,</span>
<span class="line">And sour whey may lurk in depths beneath</span>
<span class="line">The cream of goodness in a placid mind.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Since this is so, I shall not risk a test</span>
<span class="line">And lose you quite. Indeed there is no need.</span>
<span class="line">Still on your beauty shall my senses feed,</span>
<span class="line">While from the best ingredients of dessert</span>
<span class="line">In me I shall create blood, soul, and heart,</span>
<span class="line">An inner you, as perfect as the rest.</span></p>
