<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Let her whose flesh was flame</span>
<span class="line">Not rot, but burn,</span>
<span class="line">Nor let the dross lie tame</span>
<span class="line">Inside an urn.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">May wind and sun and air</span>
<span class="line">The ash assume</span>
<span class="line">That living could not bear</span>
<span class="line">A narrow room.</span></p>
