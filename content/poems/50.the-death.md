<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Eight hours a night beside her bed</span>
<span class="line">I sat and thought. Sometimes I read.</span>
<span class="line">I had no heart at all to pray</span>
<span class="line">As morphine soothed her life away,</span>
<span class="line">But in rare intervals of pain</span>
<span class="line">Her hand gripped mine, a child's again.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">At last her breathing stopped. She died.</span>
<span class="line">My giving done, I walked outside</span>
<span class="line">And felt the world's vacuity</span>
<span class="line">Merge with the emptiness in me.</span></p>
