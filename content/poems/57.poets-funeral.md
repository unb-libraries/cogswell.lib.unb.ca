<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Beyond the chapel door two pipers played </span>
<span class="line">A coronach whose sobbing grace-notes filled </span>
<span class="line">The air with feelings deeper than mere words </span>
<span class="line">Could plumb. Although your bier was still outside, </span>
<span class="line">You yet were with us. Inside our minds, deep in, </span>
<span class="line">All thoughts revolved around your central sun.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Inside, your wife, Claudine, and John, your son, </span>
<span class="line">Sat on a front pew where the sunlight played </span>
<span class="line">Strange patterns on each grief-drawn face; and in </span>
<span class="line">The seats behind, all places, too, were filled </span>
<span class="line">By friends who felt the self you were inside </span>
<span class="line">Meant more to them than any of your words.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Your coffin then arrived and, afterwards,</span>
<span class="line">All, standing, bowed in silent orison.</span>
<span class="line">The premier and the playwright friend, beside,</span>
<span class="line">In turn pronounced a eulogy and played</span>
<span class="line">Upon our feelings' strings with praise heart-filled</span>
<span class="line">That echoed all the griefs we kept held in.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">James Stewart last, a boon companion in </span>
<span class="line">The spending of good drink and better words, </span>
<span class="line">Played one full dirge upon his flute and filled </span>
<span class="line">The chapel with a final benison, </span>
<span class="line">Then snapped it off against his knee and laid </span>
<span class="line">It silent on the bier, close by your side.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Beside the river on a low hill's side</span>
<span class="line">There lay the grave we put your coffin in;</span>
<span class="line">Poured Irish, Scottish soil, libations; laid</span>
<span class="line">Still more well-meaning wreaths of flowered words,</span>
<span class="line">And took our turn with shovels until your son</span>
<span class="line">Pronounced your burial wishes all fulfilled.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The tiny house you lived in was quite filled</span>
<span class="line">With guests of all degrees, crammed side by side,</span>
<span class="line">As food and drink flowed free as air and sun,</span>
<span class="line">And all about the place, outside and in,</span>
<span class="line">The dam of silence broke and guests found words</span>
<span class="line">For your own worth and worth your work displayed.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">With fit rites and words in season, we filled</span>
<span class="line">Through you our own death-wish and left your fireside</span>
<span class="line">Remembering all the times you'd let us in.</span></p>
