<p class="stanza"><span class="line">sheer energy in a skin of form</span>
<span class="line">dances to an ever-being rhythm</span>
<span class="line">in golden rays of shimmering light</span>
<span class="line">against black walls of space and time</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">something there is akin to light</span>
<span class="line">that drives apart all heaviness</span>
<span class="line">from gravid tug and viscous flow</span>
<span class="line">to curling wave and buoyant air</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">the breeze that rocks the spider's web</span>
<span class="line">and blows aloft the thistle-seed</span>
<span class="line">is planted in the flow of sap</span>
<span class="line">and in the pulse of driven blood</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">many rhythms dance the universe;</span>
<span class="line">in light all sex and seasons wave</span>
<span class="line">whose flux contains the teeming life</span>
<span class="line">of time slow-measured by earth's spin</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">a dance analogous to mind,</span>
<span class="line">that light of self whose nimble thought</span>
<span class="line">through a maze of shifting words must mock</span>
<span class="line">the planet which it warms and feeds</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">to grasp life's total paradigm</span>
<span class="line">throw wide your window to the night</span>
<span class="line">and watch, to star-tunes echoed here,</span>
<span class="line">gold-shoes beat on a floor of ebony</span></p>
