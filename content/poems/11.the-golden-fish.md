<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The golden fish beneath the stones</span>
<span class="line">That line the bottom of the lake</span>
<span class="line">Will never rise to greet my will</span>
<span class="line">Although I stir the surface up</span>
<span class="line">With hollow reeds of sight and sound;</span>
<span class="line">But when I drown myself in sleep</span>
<span class="line">They dart alive and ripple through</span>
<span class="line">The shining waters of my dreams.</span></p>
