<p class="stanza"><span class="line">We cannot see the stars tonight; the sky</span>
<span class="line">Is black, and from the marsh across the way</span>
<span class="line">The salt wind sweeps toward the sea a spray</span>
<span class="line">Of summer rain. We watch it, you and I,</span>
<span class="line">inside the old verandah, warm and dry,</span>
<span class="line">In chairs relaxed, completely at ease,</span>
<span class="line">And warm with blankets wrapped around our knees</span>
<span class="line">Cocooned in silence till the storm blows by.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Then all at once the night grows luminous</span>
<span class="line">As a host of fire-flies in nuptial swarm</span>
<span class="line">Flash intermittent point of cold gold fire;</span>
<span class="line">And we draw closer, sensing how in us</span>
<span class="line">The same lights burn, lit by the same desire,</span>
<span class="line">Although ours are invisible and warm.</span></p>
