<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Life has a use for evil in its flow, </span>
<span class="line">Combining opposites while atoms run. </span>
<span class="line">Dung on the roses makes the roses grow.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">From rank effluvia, gross roots and low </span>
<span class="line">Suck at the soil until their thirst is gone. </span>
<span class="line">Life has a use for evil in its flow;</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">It turns destroying flame to warm hearth-glow, </span>
<span class="line">Making a second day when day is done.</span>
<span class="line">Dung on the roses makes the roses grow:</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Great blooms will die and rot as, feeding, go </span>
<span class="line">Small flies with wings like life-spans finely spun. </span>
<span class="line">Life has a use for evil in its flow;</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Its seas sustain the cells that pulse below;</span>
<span class="line">Ours live because its dark dilutes the sun;</span>
<span class="line">Dung on the roses makes the roses grow.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">This is <em>What's</em> truth whose <em>Why</em> we do not know, </span>
<span class="line">This riddle of the multiforming One: </span>
<span class="line">Life has a use for evil in its flow; </span>
<span class="line">Dung on the roses makes the roses grow.</span></p>
