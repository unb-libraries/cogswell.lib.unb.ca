<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Since I shall never have a son</span>
<span class="line">I write these words for any one</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Who for the first time knows the truth</span>
<span class="line">That hides beneath the bloom of youth</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">To plumb an awful cavity</span>
<span class="line">Between the <em>is</em> and <em>ought-to-be.</em></span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">This world that seems so fair behind</span>
<span class="line">Its veil of seeming is a blind</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Where things that should not be but are</span>
<span class="line">Assail the nose, tongue, eye, and ear</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And by their slow infection try</span>
<span class="line">To make us inmates of its sty.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">What then ought you to do? Decline</span>
<span class="line">The challenge and go root with swine?</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Or weep into your tavern beer</span>
<span class="line">To mourn the fates that brought you here?</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Or prematurely end the game</span>
<span class="line">Whose dice are not the dice they claim?</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Avoid such cop-outs, then, my son.</span>
<span class="line">Do what I and better men have done.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Like you, both they and I saw through</span>
<span class="line">The veil that blurs the false and true;</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Saw, too, the world of grace and sin</span>
<span class="line">As paradigm for ours within;</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Saw in this power to know and see</span>
<span class="line">The germ of inner victory</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And built a citadel of self</span>
<span class="line">With quality as basic wealth,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And from this base we outward strove</span>
<span class="line">To aid the good we prized through love.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">We faced grim foes that tried our grit;</span>
<span class="line">Defeated oft, we never quit,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">But raised our standards proud and high</span>
<span class="line">Against all mediocrity</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">To learn beyond all bitterness</span>
<span class="line">That winning, losing matter less.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">It is the joy of battle-glow</span>
<span class="line">That sets adrenalins aflow</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And gives a tang and zest to life</span>
<span class="line">That more than justifies the strife.</span></p>
