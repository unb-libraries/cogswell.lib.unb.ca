<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Though once a slave before tradition's throne,</span>
<span class="line">The poet now need please himself alone.</span>
<span class="line">No more by patient toil, as Horace schools,</span>
<span class="line">He serves a stern apprenticeship to rules.</span>
<span class="line">A youth, new bitten by the flea to write,</span>
<span class="line">Can scratch himself to poet overnight!</span>
<span class="line">Pebbles that lurk beneath the self's dark sea</span>
<span class="line">Can win acclaim as pearls of poetry,</span>
<span class="line">Provided that the ropes on which they're strung</span>
<span class="line">Are twisted dreams by Freud or myths by Jung;</span>
<span class="line">For, like high priests whose revenue depends</span>
<span class="line">Upon a lore no layman comprehends,</span>
<span class="line">The modern critic gives his best reviews</span>
<span class="line">To work that's esoteric and abstruse,</span>
<span class="line">And should it prove too dense (like courtier bold</span>
<span class="line">Who praised the king's new clothes in fable old)</span>
<span class="line">Boldly he'll vaunt the poem's well-cut shape</span>
<span class="line">And trace rich patterns where the public gape...</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Oh, for an honest child to cry halloo</span>
<span class="line">For naked truths that Burke and Johnson knew,</span>
<span class="line">Or another Pope from Twickenham</span>
<span class="line">To drop clean acid from a poisoned pen.</span></p>
