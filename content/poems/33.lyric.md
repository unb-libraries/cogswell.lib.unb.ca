<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Be patient, Sweet. Forbear</span>
<span class="line">Your power to keep me true.</span>
<span class="line">O let me laugh a while</span>
<span class="line">With warmer loves than you,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">That I may tire at last</span>
<span class="line">Of change which will not cease</span>
<span class="line">And long to trade life's charms</span>
<span class="line">For your one gift of peace.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Come then with kiss that dooms</span>
<span class="line">All other loves that call,</span>
<span class="line">And glad in your embrace</span>
<span class="line">I shall forget them all.</span></p>
