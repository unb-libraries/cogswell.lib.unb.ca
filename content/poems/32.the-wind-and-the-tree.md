<p class="stanza"><span class="line">That Spring the gnarled and ancient apple tree,</span>
<span class="line">Feeling the May wind's kiss, unfurled</span>
<span class="line">Green leaves like flags to greet the sun, and all</span>
<span class="line">Its crown grew white with blossom where great bees</span>
<span class="line">With pollen-dusted fur flew day-long flights</span>
<span class="line">To hive the harvest of the scented boughs.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Now on the Autumn ground the apples lie,</span>
<span class="line">And with them soon will mingle in the mould</span>
<span class="line">The leaves whose edges even now are brown</span>
<span class="line">With death; for deadlier than the keenest axe</span>
<span class="line">The kiss of too much living drove the tree</span>
<span class="line">To greater harvest than its back could bear.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">A kissing wind that did not dream its power</span>
<span class="line">A tree that could not feel the limits of</span>
<span class="line">Its all too feeble strength -- by chance the two</span>
<span class="line">Combined. The end their union formed was fruit.</span>
<span class="line">But which of us who dreams and feels can say</span>
<span class="line">Whether the end of fruit be life or death?</span></p>
