<p class="stanza"><span class="line">O narrow is the house where we are born,</span>
<span class="line">And narrow are the fields in which we labour,</span>
<span class="line">Fenced in by rails and woods that low hills neighbour</span>
<span class="line">Lest they should spill their crops of hay and corn.</span>
<span class="line">O narrow are the hates with which we thorn</span>
<span class="line">Each other's flesh by gossip of the Grundies,</span>
<span class="line">And narrow are our roads to church on Sundays,</span>
<span class="line">And narrow too the vows of love we've sworn.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">But through our fields the Saint John river flows</span>
<span class="line">And mocks the patterned fields that we enclose;</span>
<span class="line">There sometimes pausing in the dusty heat</span>
<span class="line">We stretch cramped backs and lean upon our hoes</span>
<span class="line">To watch a sea-gull glide with lazy beat</span>
<span class="line">To wider regions where the river goes.</span></p>
