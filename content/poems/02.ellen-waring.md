<p class="stanza"><span class="line">A fruitful tree of life is Ellen Waring;</span>
<span class="line">Where others of her kind bear when they suit,</span>
<span class="line">She pays her husband's toil with yearly fruit,</span>
<span class="line">Firm-fleshed as flesh from which it takes its faring.</span>
<span class="line">But now her once-smooth skin in spots is wearing</span>
<span class="line">Rough bark that only hands of death can smooth;</span>
<span class="line">Her trunk and limbs grow gnarled and stout to prove</span>
<span class="line">The tree that bears must pay the price of bearing.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">She is not so unto her husband's sight.</span>
<span class="line">The light through which he sees is filtered by</span>
<span class="line">The colours of one warm September sky</span>
<span class="line">When she, on their first morning of delight,</span>
<span class="line">Stood bare in limbs and body's symmetry,</span>
<span class="line">As lissom-lovely as a poplar tree.</span></p>
