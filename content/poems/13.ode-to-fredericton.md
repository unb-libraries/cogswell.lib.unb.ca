book: "Descent from Eden"
bookOrder: 3
oldPath: "/odetofredericton"
---
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">White are your housetops, white too your vaulted elms</span>
<span class="line">That make your stately streets long aisles of prayer,</span>
<span class="line">And white your thirteen spires that point your God</span>
<span class="line">Who reigns afar in pure and whiter air,</span>
<span class="line">And white the dome of your democracy-</span>
<span class="line">The snow has pitied you and made you fair,</span>
<span class="line">O snow-washed city of cold, white Christians,</span>
<span class="line">So white you will not cut a black man's hair.</span></p>
