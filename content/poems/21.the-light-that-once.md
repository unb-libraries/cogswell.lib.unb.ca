<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The light that once in prehistoric seas</span>
<span class="line">Propelled the lancing sperm</span>
<span class="line">Is poisoned by the cells' disease</span>
<span class="line">To feed the wars of rose and worm.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Then do not wake to light the sleeping girl,</span>
<span class="line">Nor rouse her sea-salt blood;</span>
<span class="line">Still round her loins let fig-leaves curl,</span>
<span class="line">Let angels guard her dreams from flood.</span></p>
