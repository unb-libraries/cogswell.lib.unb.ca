<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Here in our town she bloomed, a scarlet rose</span>
<span class="line">Among the other girls, and all the men</span>
<span class="line">Who ever looked went crazy for her then,</span>
<span class="line">Like bees that haunt the place where honey grows.</span>
<span class="line">But when she left to dance in burlesque shows,</span>
<span class="line">As shifty as the snow across the track</span>
<span class="line">Of deer, the rumours of her life blew back,</span>
<span class="line">Though what the wind that blew them no one knows.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And slowly to us boys and girls her case</span>
<span class="line">Became a legend of iniquity;</span>
<span class="line">So when her father died and Rose came back</span>
<span class="line">We watched with awe and curiosity</span>
<span class="line">A thin, tall scare-crow dressed in faded black</span>
<span class="line">With tears that trickled down her withered face.</span></p>
