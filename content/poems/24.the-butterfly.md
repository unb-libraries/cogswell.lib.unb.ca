<p class="stanza"><span class="line">For years I hunted butterflies and looked with pride</span>
<span class="line">on rows of set and labelled wings</span>
<span class="line">that slowly faded underneath the glass.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Today by chance or whim one rare and beautiful</span>
<span class="line">swooped down to light upon my open palm.</span>
<span class="line">I did not close my fist.</span></p>
