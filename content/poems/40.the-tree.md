<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Its roots are hands to scoop up rain</span>
<span class="line">its leaves are throats that drink the sun</span>
<span class="line">these disappear when warmth is done</span>
<span class="line">the whole tree is the whole tree's brain</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">and every scar it has will heal</span>
<span class="line">no matter where the wound may be</span>
<span class="line">but anywhere upon the tree</span>
<span class="line">too great a scar will slowly kill</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">without lungs it breathes but has no breath</span>
<span class="line">without a heart its thin blood flows</span>
<span class="line">with no eyes it light and darkness knows</span>
<span class="line">and it knows too of coming death</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">for feeling in its wounded cells</span>
<span class="line">will cause a crippled tree to fling</span>
<span class="line">more blossoms to the winds of spring</span>
<span class="line">than one whom youthful sap impels</span></p>
