book: "Descent from Eden"

oldPath: "/dragontree"
---
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Strange-scented birds and song-flowers grow</span>
<span class="line">In the garden where I cannot go,</span>
<span class="line">Where green-trunked trees grey apples hold</span>
<span class="line">And blue fish swim in pools of gold.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And always there a green sun glows</span>
<span class="line">To burn the song of the red-leafed rose,</span>
<span class="line">While yellow grasses bend their knees</span>
<span class="line">Before a bluebird-smelling breeze.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Around the garden's circle flies</span>
<span class="line">The dragon-tree to eat the skies</span>
<span class="line">With silver-scented fruit that sings</span>
<span class="line">Hid in the branches of its wings.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">That garden now to me is gone</span>
<span class="line">Where sight and sound and sense are one,</span>
<span class="line">But children walk there still before</span>
<span class="line">They eat the dragon's cherished store.</span></p>
