<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The berries on the rowan brightly glow </span>
<span class="line">Against the green in bunches on the tree. </span>
<span class="line">They'll feed the wandering birds in winter-time </span>
<span class="line">When snow is deep and sun its warmth resigns, </span>
<span class="line">Making these thieves, alert for fruit and seeds, </span>
<span class="line">Unwitting partners in the rowan's spread.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The berries on the rowan, thickly spread, </span>
<span class="line">Are never so inviting as their glow. </span>
<span class="line">In autumn, birds refuse them and their seeds </span>
<span class="line">Are left for months uneaten on the tree.</span>
<span class="line"> When winter brings starvation, taste resigns </span>
<span class="line">And grosbeaks pounce on them at feeding time.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The berries on the rowan know no time.</span>
<span class="line">Passive themselves, on wings of birds they spread</span>
<span class="line">Wherever birds can fly. Their fruit consigns</span>
<span class="line">Its bitter sustenance when food is low;</span>
<span class="line">For this, whole flocks fly errands to the tree,</span>
<span class="line">Then find new ground on which to drop its seeds.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The berries on the rowan bear the seeds </span>
<span class="line">Of magic passed to us from olden time. </span>
<span class="line">Against all evil spirits is the tree </span>
<span class="line">On guard, but most of all its clustered spread </span>
<span class="line">Defeats dark elves. They never pass the glow </span>
<span class="line">Red rowans make, despite their dark designs.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The berries on the rowan are the signs </span>
<span class="line">Of faith the tree has in its fruit and seeds. </span>
<span class="line">The blood of life is red within their glow. </span>
<span class="line">The bread of life is in the fruit that time </span>
<span class="line">Will take into the maws of birds and spread </span>
<span class="line">To resurrect from it a seedling tree.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The berries on the rowan are the tree</span>
<span class="line">That every fall its energy consigns</span>
<span class="line">To crowning boughs with clusters thickly spread</span>
<span class="line">Of thin and tasteless fruit and pellet seeds.</span>
<span class="line">This providence is somehow linked to time</span>
<span class="line">And starving birds that glimpse a scarlet glow.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Each year when autumn colours glow, the tree</span>
<span class="line">Puts forth its seeds and after that resigns</span>
<span class="line">To time and chance the manner of their spread.</span></p>
