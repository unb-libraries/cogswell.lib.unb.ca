<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Love on my part</span>
<span class="line">Is sweet, absurd;</span>
<span class="line">It makes my heart</span>
<span class="line">A crazy bird</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">That now must sing</span>
<span class="line">A springtime song</span>
<span class="line">Though every Spring</span>
<span class="line">Has come and gone.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Let it sing clear</span>
<span class="line">Both night and day...</span>
<span class="line">Its notes may scare</span>
<span class="line">Time's chill away.</span></p>
