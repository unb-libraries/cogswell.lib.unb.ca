<p class="stanza"><span class="line">though quazars pierce an emptiness</span>
<span class="line">six billion light years cannot hold</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">though light itself is trapped</span>
<span class="line">in the cold of collapsed nebulae</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">though all of history</span>
<span class="line">is dwarfed by spatial sands</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">still...</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">the earthworm glistens in the dew</span>
<span class="line">its beauty independent of</span>
<span class="line">the hunger of a bird</span></p>
