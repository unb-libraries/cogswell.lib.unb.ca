<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Big Cyrus in the old religion trod.</span>
<span class="line">He was no reed whom winds of pleasure bowed;</span>
<span class="line">With face as stony as the land he plowed</span>
<span class="line">He read his Bible and he loved his God.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Scanning the heaven's face for frown or nod,</span>
<span class="line">He watched his Master like a beaten dog,</span>
<span class="line">But not a glimmer pierced the aweful fog</span>
<span class="line">To show a clear intent - reward or rod.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And then he met a girl and thought the sign</span>
<span class="line">That showed him numbered with the saints divine</span>
<span class="line">Was in her love, but that was clothed in flesh</span>
<span class="line">And flesh he knew was sin; caught in the mesh</span>
<span class="line">Of indecision still he lingered on</span>
<span class="line">Nor made his mind up till his chance was gone.</span></p>
