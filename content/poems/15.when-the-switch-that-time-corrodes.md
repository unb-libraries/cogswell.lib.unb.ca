<p class="stanza"><span class="line">When the switch that time corrodes</span>
<span class="line">clicks hard against the will</span>
<span class="line">and a flash-back bulb explodes</span>
<span class="line">A long-forgotten cell,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">flesh that died two decades back</span>
<span class="line">reactivates its pain;</span>
<span class="line">in the room my mind kept black</span>
<span class="line">light breaks the heart again</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">to show through gathering dust</span>
<span class="line">that thick on the image lies</span>
<span class="line">the face of my ruined lust,</span>
<span class="line">the hell in her tortured eyes.</span></p>
