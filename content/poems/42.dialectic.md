<p class="stanza"><span class="line">I bear a god within the brain</span>
<span class="line">that beasts know nothing of</span>
<span class="line">It makes me give against the grain</span>
<span class="line">in the very name of love</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">By it the infant and the old,</span>
<span class="line">the halt, diseased, insane</span>
<span class="line">are kept from violence, hunger, cold</span>
<span class="line">that in the beast-world reign</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">But I at best am centaur still,</span>
<span class="line">god-vision trapped in flesh,</span>
<span class="line">my selfish heart defeats my will,</span>
<span class="line">my mindless loins unleash</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Now in the haunts of passion slain</span>
<span class="line">its flesh become a clod</span>
<span class="line">the beast is sleeping, and in its brain</span>
<span class="line">revives the fallen god</span></p>
