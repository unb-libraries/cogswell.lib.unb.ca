<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Under</span>
<span class="line">the willow where</span>
<span class="line">the falling pollen</span>
<span class="line">powdered your face</span>
<span class="line">I think of the first</span>
<span class="line">wind that blew</span>
<span class="line">on the first willow</span>
<span class="line">by this lake</span></p>

<span class="line">brief embrace</span></p>

<span class="line">remember her ghostly father</span>
<span class="line">as she feeds</span>
<span class="line">on her mother's mould</span></p>
