<p class="stanza"><span class="line">There are two worlds in time; one dark, one light:</span>
<span class="line">In circling stasis each on other preys,</span>
<span class="line">Where all between the poles of black and white</span>
<span class="line">Revolve or spiral through their nights and days.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">When you behold the blind and silent worm</span>
<span class="line">Become a song inside a robin's throat,</span>
<span class="line">Or see in Spring an orchid blossom form</span>
<span class="line">Its own sweet scent from slime that feeds its root,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Think how in turn a worm will feed as well,</span>
<span class="line">One night to come, upon that robin's meat,</span>
<span class="line">Or how the root that gave will cannibal</span>
<span class="line">And care not if the bloom be foul or sweet;</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And realize, then, unlike flower, worm, bird, slime,</span>
<span class="line">Your thoughts of time have put you outside time.</span></p>
