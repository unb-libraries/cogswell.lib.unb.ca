<p class="stanza"><span class="line">in a hospital</span>
<span class="line">a breath of infant breath blends</span>
<span class="line">with a last-gasp death</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">the child does not know</span>
<span class="line">he is alive nor the man</span>
<span class="line">that his breathing's done</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">nor can those watchers</span>
<span class="line">who pronounce that one is dead</span>
<span class="line">and the other born</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">say with certainty</span>
<span class="line">of what they saw before them</span>
<span class="line">any more than this</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">"in a hospital</span>
<span class="line">we watched two breaths meet in time</span>
<span class="line">the rest in silence"</span></p>
