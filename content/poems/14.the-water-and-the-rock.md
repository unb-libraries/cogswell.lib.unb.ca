<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Hard rock was I, and she was water flowing,</span>
<span class="line">Over sharp stones of opposition going;</span>
<span class="line">Shaping herself to me as to a cup,</span>
<span class="line">She filled the valleys of my ego up</span>
<span class="line">With a cool, smooth compliance, everywhere</span>
<span class="line">As yielding and unhurtable as air.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Soft was my love as water, and I forgot</span>
<span class="line">In the calm wash of complaint rhythm caught</span>
<span class="line">How water shapes and softens, sculpts and smooths</span>
<span class="line">The channel of the rock through which it moves.</span></p>
