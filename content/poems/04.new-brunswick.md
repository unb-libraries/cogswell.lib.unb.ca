<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Before it takes the air in greener shoots</span>
<span class="line">A seed is nurtured by surrounding soil</span>
<span class="line">And patterned by whatever streams can coil</span>
<span class="line">Where worms and borers worked their slow pursuits;</span>
<span class="line">And though it wills to grow a crown that fruits</span>
<span class="line">In skies where lightnings break and thunders clap,</span>
<span class="line">It can't escape the source that feeds its sap:</span>
<span class="line">No tree belies its soil, outgrows its roots.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Not soft the soil where we took root together;</span>
<span class="line">It grew not giants but the stunted strong,</span>
<span class="line">Toughened by suns and bleak wintry weather</span>
<span class="line">To grow up slow and to endure for long;</span>
<span class="line">We have not gained to any breadth or length,</span>
<span class="line">And all our beauty is our stubborn strength.</span></p>
