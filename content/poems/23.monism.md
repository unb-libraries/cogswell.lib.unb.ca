<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Where river slides through spreading thighs of soil</span>
<span class="line">Whose maidenhead is captured by the worm,</span>
<span class="line">Where summer air wins free from tension's coil</span>
<span class="line">As lightning darts to deepest womb of storm</span>
<span class="line">Its thrust of terror and of beauty home,</span>
<span class="line">I look around me and my senses reel</span>
<span class="line">To see the universe outside become</span>
<span class="line">A mirror of the passion that I feel.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Where jetting fountains into basins run,</span>
<span class="line">Where mountains thrust hard heads through yielding cloud,</span>
<span class="line">Where Lombard poplars straighten in the sun,</span>
<span class="line">Where grass springs phallic from the fleshly shroud</span>
<span class="line">And stubborn root rapes bone till bone is gone,</span>
<span class="line">The world grows crystal clear in terms of form</span>
<span class="line">As micro-macrocosmic shapes make one</span>
<span class="line">Recurring symbol of the mated norm.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">And there stream, worm, air, tree, mount, grass, and root</span>
<span class="line">Am I; you are all complements to these;</span>
<span class="line">And each relation, articulate or mute,</span>
<span class="line">Is ours; and should death end our entities</span>
<span class="line">These cells in other forms will dance the same;</span>
<span class="line">Whether in galaxies or the mind's rooms</span>
<span class="line">In unity unfolds the endless game</span>
<span class="line">Of egg-and-dart all energy assumes.</span></p>
