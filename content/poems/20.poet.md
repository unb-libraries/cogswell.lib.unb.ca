<p class="stanza"><span class="line">desperate for balance</span>
<span class="line">he walked with his hands</span>
<span class="line">a paper treadmill</span>
<span class="line">over the edge</span>
<span class="line">of his topsy-turvy life</span>
<span class="line">and dared not pause</span>
<span class="line">lest he fall</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">we who see only</span>
<span class="line">his marks on the paper</span>
<span class="line">praise him for energy</span>
<span class="line">find in his agility</span>
<span class="line">the rhythms of a happy dance</span></p>
