<p class="stanza"><span class="line">I know four girls who might make queens</span>
<span class="line">In timeless Avalon, and they</span>
<span class="line">And I have had our fill of this</span>
<span class="line">Clock-heavy world. But I, alas,</span>
<span class="line">Am neither Arthur nor a king.</span>
<span class="line">Nor is there magic anywhere.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">But let's not cry on that account.</span>
<span class="line">Clasp hands, shut eyes to form a ring,</span>
<span class="line">And dance until we all fall down.</span>
<span class="line">Perhaps a dizzy spell will do</span>
<span class="line">What magic can't and in a trice</span>
<span class="line">Swing wide the singing gates of gold.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Out from that burst of vertigo</span>
<span class="line">Which numbs the sense of things that are</span>
<span class="line">We five will sally forth and seize</span>
<span class="line">The dazzling sands that lie between</span>
<span class="line">The orchard bowers of idleness</span>
<span class="line">And the tough, salt-muscled sea.</span></p>
