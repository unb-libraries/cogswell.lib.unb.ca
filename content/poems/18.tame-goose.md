<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Down high ways of heaven wild geese fly</span>
<span class="line">the tame goose answers with a cry</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">along a ring of palings high</span>
<span class="line">she stretches neck and runs to try</span>
<span class="line">the mocking wings she can't control</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">but when the goose-girl brings the rye</span>
<span class="line">she snatches up and gobbles whole</span>
<span class="line">the grain safe nestling in the bowl</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">nor heeds the whistling wings gone by.</span></p>
