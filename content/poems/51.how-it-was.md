<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The road we travelled was the usual one</span>
<span class="line">Uphill and down it wound its way between</span>
<span class="line">Pale fields of grain and woods of darker green,</span>
<span class="line">Skirting the river where cool waters run,</span>
<span class="line">Glassy and blue beneath a summer sun.</span>
<span class="line">Along the way, scarce noticing, we passed</span>
<span class="line">Houses, barns, pastures thick with cows. At last</span>
<span class="line">We stopped before a graveyard, journey done.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">At her grandparents' tomb I took a spade</span>
<span class="line">Turned up the sod, and put her urn beneath</span>
<span class="line">To be with them in place and time and death. </span>
<span class="line">Then as I covered up the hole I made</span>
<span class="line">My ears were opened and I heard the strong</span>
<span class="line">And living sweetness of a robin's song.</span></p>
