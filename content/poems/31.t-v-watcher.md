<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Soft in her chair she sees the visions bloom</span>
<span class="line">And needs no prince to wake her drowsy flesh.</span>
<span class="line">Soft on her screen she sees the patterns mesh</span>
<span class="line">Of rugged males whose scowling passions loom</span>
<span class="line">With two-gunned violence in her living-room.</span>
<span class="line">Oh, how she loves them, aches to share their throes,</span>
<span class="line">Her feelings sharp and variable as those</span>
<span class="line">Who move in monochrome a switch can doom.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">She will not flick it off for flesh and blood.</span>
<span class="line">Where can she give so little, gain so much,</span>
<span class="line">Be heroic without risk, diet without food,</span>
<span class="line">Adventure safely in the bleak outdoors,</span>
<span class="line">Have lovely children without household chores,</span>
<span class="line">And thrill to love untarnished by a touch?</span></p>
