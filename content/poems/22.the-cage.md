<p class="stanza"><span class="line">At times the bird inside the cage</span>
<span class="line">Is very dear to me.</span>
<span class="line">At times my thoughts go with the bird</span>
<span class="line">That flies alone and free.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Should I let loose the bird I have</span>
<span class="line">To coax the wild one here</span>
<span class="line">Such change of roles would only make</span>
<span class="line">The other one more dear.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Without the cage I cannot want</span>
<span class="line">Or love or think or say;</span>
<span class="line">Its barriers limn the forms of life</span>
<span class="line">As sun-spokes shape the day.</span></p>
