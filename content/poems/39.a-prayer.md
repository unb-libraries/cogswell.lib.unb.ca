<p class="stanza"><span class="line">O You, whose loving laws designed</span>
<span class="line">The knots that men and women hold,</span>
<span class="line">Lord of the hearth and home, be kind</span>
<span class="line">To all who chafe in bands of gold </span>–</p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Poor fools who walk staid roads and crave</span>
<span class="line">The high, bird-travelled sky above,</span>
<span class="line">Who want the things they dare not have</span>
<span class="line">And have the things they will not love.</span></p>
