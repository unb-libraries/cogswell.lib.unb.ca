<p class="stanza"><span class="line">All the years I hunted butterflies</span>
<span class="line">In field and wood and lane</span>
<span class="line">I saw but once a phaeton</span>
<span class="line">Perched on a blue vervain.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Slow I crept up near as could be,</span>
<span class="line">So tense I scarcely breathed,</span>
<span class="line">Then swiftly swung my trusty net.</span>
<span class="line">In gauze it was ensheathed.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">It hung a score of years, the pride</span>
<span class="line">Of my collection</span>
<span class="line">Till time and parasites combined</span>
<span class="line">For its destruction.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">But I know now that while I live</span>
<span class="line">I never shall forget</span>
<span class="line">The intense and awful joy I felt</span>
<span class="line">When I caught it in my net.</span></p>
