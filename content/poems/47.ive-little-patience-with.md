<p class="stanza"><span class="line">I've little patience with those friends of mine</span>
<span class="line">Who say that life is naught since all men die -</span>
<span class="line">Who would the tavern's richest juice decline</span>
<span class="line">Because the tavern closes bye and bye?</span></p>
