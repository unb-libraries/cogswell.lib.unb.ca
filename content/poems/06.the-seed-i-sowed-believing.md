<p class="stanza"><span class="line">The seed I sowed, believing</span>
<span class="line">In an everlasting rose,</span>
<span class="line">Is now a stalk, unleaving</span>
<span class="line">Its harvest to the snows,</span>
<span class="line">And from its green-leafed fire</span>
<span class="line">There's only left to burn</span>
<span class="line">The stiff bone of the briar,</span>
<span class="line">The cold-glossed hollow thorn.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Though bitter sap is squeezing</span>
<span class="line">Where once a sweet glow ran,</span>
<span class="line">My twisted stalk is seizing</span>
<span class="line">These comforts while it can:</span>
<span class="line">Although no bud shall splinter</span>
<span class="line">Fresh leaves to greet the spring,</span>
<span class="line">The briar will last out winter,</span>
<span class="line">The thorn be sharp to sting.</span></p>
