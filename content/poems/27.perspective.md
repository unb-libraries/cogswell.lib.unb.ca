<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Like a moth whose vision dwindles</span>
<span class="line">To one clear point of fire</span>
<span class="line">My passion bent the universe</span>
<span class="line">To the form of its desire.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Now free at last I gaze, my eye</span>
<span class="line">Content to look alone:</span>
<span class="line">How lovely is the varied world</span>
<span class="line">I have no wish to own.</span></p>
