<p class="stanza"><span class="line">I shall not when I die</span>
<span class="line">Quake for hell-fire</span>
<span class="line">Nor dream with desire</span>
<span class="line">Of a celestial sky,</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">But rather grieve with pain -</span>
<span class="line">Still thinking on</span>
<span class="line">The things I have done</span>
<span class="line">And may not do again.</span></p>
