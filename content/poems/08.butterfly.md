<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Bedraggled now and crushed,</span>
<span class="line">frail soul of fluttering things,</span>
<span class="line">forgive my hands that brushed</span>
<span class="line">the sun dust from your wings;</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">for though I turn and twist</span>
<span class="line">in weak attempts to flee,</span>
<span class="line">hard hands I can't resist</span>
<span class="line">shall do the same to me.</span></p>
