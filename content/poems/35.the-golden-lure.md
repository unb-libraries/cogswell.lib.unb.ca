<p class="stanza"><span class="line">An epicure will watch with glee</span>
<span class="line">a dying fish, and thrill to know</span>
<span class="line">what living colours ebb and flow</span>
<span class="line">from a heart that bursts in agony.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">Like him, your appetite is art</span>
<span class="line">where passion makes a daintier dish</span>
<span class="line">each time, as in that rainbow fish,</span>
<span class="line">it blazes from a broken heart.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">So when, a golden lure, you break</span>
<span class="line">the liquid surface of my eye,</span>
<span class="line">the foolish fish of joy awake</span>
<span class="line">then dart beneath to safety.</span></p>
<p class="stanza"><span class="line">For there's one truth I've learned holds good</span>
<span class="line">That the merely loved can never share:</span>
<span class="line">Whoever drowns in water would</span>
<span class="line">Not wish a fish to drown in air.</span></p>
